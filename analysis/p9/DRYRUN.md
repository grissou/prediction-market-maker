1. **HIGH, caveat - stage 2 frees far less than the plan's 15-20k of tilt exposure.** The taker exits only take levels within 1c of the tilted fair value. On the snapshot's books that is $3,179 of cash / 1,511 of tilt exposure at s 0.11, $8,280 / 4,055 at the estimator's reading s 0.127, $37,832 / 18,536 only if s reached 0.20. The go-condition of the plan (worst_case_loss < ~40k, cash_gate_left > ~20k) is not reached by stage 2 on these books; it needs the tilt estimate to rise (the book's longshots repricing toward Polymarket) or fresh depth. The 15k$/h and 3-per-cycle caps never bind.
2. **MEDIUM, caveat - stage 3 with the cash stage 2 really freed builds a small basket.** Switched on with 21,346 of cash: 55 legs, $30,512 held against a full target of $44,749, then 'no free cash (cash gate)'. The basket is cash-bound, not worst-case-bound (the 0.9 backstop is ~91k; the worst case stays ~75k). The leg cap (5% of the target) then concentrates it in the laggards first and the cheap favourite-NO legs (3-12c a NO share).
3. **HIGH, caveat - the CPPI target shrinks as the basket is bought.** At the go-condition (cash 91,287) the full target fell from ~62k in the first hour to $39,622 when built ($39,809 held, 64 legs): every $ bought cuts the cushion (liquidation - floor) by basket_impact_frac 0.06 plus the spread paid (bought at the ask, valued at the bid in the liquidation value, ~8-10% on 5-10c longshots), so with m 5 the build settles near m x C0 / (1 + m x 0.15) ~ 40k instead of 5 x 14.3k = 72k; stage3b (m 6, cap 90k) settles at $43,950. The catch-up odds (29-40%) were computed for a 72-90k basket: with this sizing they are lower. Not changed here (a sizing decision for the owner: e.g. measure the cushion before the basket's own spread, or raise m).
