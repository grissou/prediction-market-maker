## D. Rules and settlement (FC-9, FC-8, reader 4's quotes)
"All trades must be received prior to 12:00pm Eastern Standard Time on November 4, 2026." "Each Participant's final score/rank shall be
based on his or her verified total SUSQies balance as of the resolution of all Markets." "No tiebreakers." "Sponsor may ... update the
rules, outcomes, or resolution of a Market at any time." Nothing on independents, control, runoffs, recounts, void markets. Polymarket's
Senate rule (which our fair value inherits): independents count for the party they caucus with or announce; unannounced by 4 Jan "will not
count"; 50-50 goes to the VP's party. Late settlement: Georgia runoff 1 Dec (Governor likely), Alaska RCV ~18 Nov, Maine RCV +1-2 weeks:
delays the standings, not our balance. **Open questions for SIG:** the per-market trading close vs the 12:00 pm ET rule; how control is
decided; what happens to an unresolved market.
